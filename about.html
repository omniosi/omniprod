About Me

Ornette Coleman

UX Design Lead and Front-end Developer

Creative with Technology

I deliver website technology products, across devices, that meet business goals, are fast-loading, accessible to most users, and are high-ranking on search engines.

I lead cross-discipline teams, internal and external, to data-driven goals.

I created and launched an award-winning website landing page for a new product, Poland Spring Origin, that saw Organic Traffic rise as much as 294%.

I was part of a team that saved Nike an estimated 3,000 work hours per year from one internal technology product feature delivered.

Visual Design

I have drawn all of my life and enjoy the challenges of Character Design, where the visual details in an image tell a story.

I have a growing collection of Concept Art books and look up to Viz Dev (Visual Development) artists as I continue to improve my digital painting skills.

Sound Design

I have been sourcing and recording sounds into mixes since getting my first tape deck. I love highlighting the connections between music styles. Being named after a Jazz Musician, I am always asked if I play any instruments. While I never learned to play, I have deejayed and now create electronic music.
