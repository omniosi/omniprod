Resurrection

3D Design and Animation

An animated digital sculpture. The motion is set to the audio of "Resurrection" by Common.

Process

Created a flat design as reference in Figma. Then built the sculpture in Blender. Pulling in the audio and setting markers for animation guides, each letter was animated in Blender.

Process
